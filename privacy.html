HunterArsenal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Summary

HunterArsenal does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. HunterArsenal does not send any information over the internet.

What is stored on your device

Your habits, check-ins, progress, skills, records, settings, Daily Mission schedule, and the optional profile details you enter (name, birth date, and profile photo). This information is stored in HunterArsenal’s private app storage, which other apps cannot read. HunterArsenal also keeps automatic local backup copies of your progress in the same private storage.

App Lock and biometrics

If you turn on App Lock, HunterArsenal stores a protected form of your passcode or password on your device, never the passcode itself. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled entirely by Android; HunterArsenal never receives or stores biometric data.

Backups you export

When you export a backup, HunterArsenal saves a file to the location you choose. Encrypted backups are protected by the passphrase you set. Plain backups are not encrypted. You control where these files go and who can see them.

Permissions

Display over other apps: shows your Daily Mission HUD on top of other apps at the time you schedule. Notifications: Daily Mission alerts, reminders, and the standby notice that keeps scheduled missions on time. Alarms and reminders: delivers missions at the exact minute you choose. Run at startup: restores your mission schedule after the phone restarts. Biometric: optional fingerprint or face unlock for App Lock. None of these permissions are used to collect information.

Children

HunterArsenal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Data & Storage → Reset all data, or uninstall HunterArsenal. Uninstalling removes all app data from your device; export a backup first if you want to keep your progress.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app with a new date.
