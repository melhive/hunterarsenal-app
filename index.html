INSTALLGet HunterArsenal on your phone.

  1. Download the app with the button below. Your browser may say this type of file can harm your device; that is the standard warning for any app downloaded outside an app store. Tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager.
  3. Install and open HunterArsenal. The System will take it from there.
  4. For Daily Missions on Infinix, TECNO, and itel phones: in Phone Master, turn on Auto-start (App launch) for HunterArsenal so missions arrive on time.

FAQQuestions

Is HunterArsenal free?

Yes. Download it and use every feature. There are no ads and no purchases.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No. It works completely offline and has no account. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays on your phone, in storage other apps cannot read. You can add App Lock and save encrypted backups. Because nothing is stored online, save a backup before you change phones or uninstall.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 6.0 or newer. Some phones (for example Infinix, TECNO, and itel) stop apps in the background; turn on Auto-start for HunterArsenal so Daily Missions arrive on time. The app's System Setup screen walks you through it.

How do I update?

Download the latest version from this page and install it over the one you have. Your progress is kept.

Is it medical or fitness advice?

No. HunterArsenal is a motivation tool for building habits. Talk to a professional before starting a new exercise or health program.
